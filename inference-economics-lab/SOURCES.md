## Methodological warning

Published token tariffs do not reveal model-level provider COGS. Hardware cost, fleet utilization, batching, quantization, speculative decoding, latency SLOs, negotiated rates, and cost allocation are generally not public. The provider-economics sandbox is hypothetical and exposes its assumptions for this reason.
